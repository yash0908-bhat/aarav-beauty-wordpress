Service questions

Not sure where to start?

These quick answers should help you choose your next step.

How do I choose between Hair, Beauty and Wellness?
Start with the result you want. Hair is for cuts, colour and styling; Beauty covers personal beauty treatments; Wellness is for a more relaxed self-care experience.
Can I book directly from this page?
Yes. Use any Book Appointment button to move to the booking page and select your preferred service and available appointment time.
What if I do not know which exact service I need?
Visit the dedicated Hair or Beauty page for more detail, or use the Contact page to ask before booking.
Are all service details shown here?
This page gives you the main service categories. The dedicated Hair and Beauty pages are where more detailed service information can be presented.
