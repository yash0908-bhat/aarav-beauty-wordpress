Hair questions

Before you book.

A few quick answers to make choosing your hair appointment easier.

Which hair service should I choose?
Start with your main goal: Cut & Shape, Colour, Styling & Finish, or Hair Care. If you are still unsure, contact Aarav Beauty before booking.
Can I book a hair appointment online?
Yes. Use any Book Hair Appointment button to open the booking page and select an available appointment.
What if I want a colour change but do not know what to select?
Use the Contact page first and explain the result you have in mind. You can then choose the most suitable booking option.
Where will pricing and appointment options appear?
Final pricing and appointment options can be added to the booking experience once the salon's exact service menu is confirmed.
