A little help

Before you book.

A few simple answers to help you decide what to do next.

How do I make an appointment?
Use the Book Appointment button to open the booking page, choose your service and select an available time.
Can I look through services first?
Yes. Use the Services, Hair and Beauty pages to explore the available service areas before you book.
What if I am not sure what to book?
Use the Contact page to send your question before booking. You can explain what you are looking for and decide from there.
Can I contact Aarav Beauty directly?
Yes. The website includes phone and email contact details in the header, footer and Contact page.
