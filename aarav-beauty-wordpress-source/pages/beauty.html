Beauty questions

Before you book.

A few quick answers to help you choose your beauty appointment.

Which beauty service should I choose?
Start with your main goal: Skin & Care, Finishing Touches, Occasion Beauty or Personal Care. If you are still unsure, use the Contact page before booking.
Can I book a beauty appointment online?
Yes. Use any Book Beauty Appointment button to open the booking page and choose an available appointment.
What if I am preparing for a special occasion?
Start with the Occasion Beauty section. If the exact service you need is not yet listed, contact Aarav Beauty before booking.
Where will exact treatment names and pricing appear?
Exact treatments, pricing and appointment options can be added once the salon's final beauty service menu is confirmed.
